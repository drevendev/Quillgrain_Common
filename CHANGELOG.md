# Changelog

## 0.5.0 — conditional tone and coherent demonstrations

Revisit Russian tone, rhythm, explanation and scene rules after broader reference
review. A direct answer or sober notice is valid; humor and a comic ending are
optional. Distinguish factual tasks from creative-fiction briefs, and narrator
speech from quotations and character voices. Four new original synthetic
before/after scenarios replace the former default RU examples, retained as RETIRED.
English examples stay unchanged; clarify their source boundary. Preserve exact
0.4.2 data alongside 0.3.0 and pin public CI to engine 0.1.0rc4. This is a new
CANDIDATE, without inherited literary acceptance or automatic consumer upgrades.

## Repository rename and corpus migration — 2026-10-23

Renamed to Quillgrain_Voice_Common; quillgrain.common and 0.4.2 voice data are
unchanged. Optional source research lives in the separate private corpus, with no
runtime dependency. Added exact historical 0.3.0 data for explicitly pinned voices.
Refreshed engine locks for Quillgrain 0.1.0rc4; synthetic examples remain standalone.

## 0.4.2

Owner-feedback candidate: restore conversational observations and rhythm, without obligatory wit; explicitly reject unsolicited calls to action and invented reasons or reader obligations. Announcement demonstration revised; fiction demonstration unchanged.

## 0.4.1

Owner-feedback candidate: quieter factual openings and optional humor, a restrained announcement demonstration, and explicit checks for audience, actor and state. Fiction guidance and its example are unchanged. Local paired Gemma checks remain qualitative evidence.

## 0.4.0

Russian Common now defaults to concrete, conversational delivery with connected rhythm, scoped humor, distinct scene dialogue and explicit limits on borrowing source facts or punchlines. Four new original synthetic demonstrations replace the default RU examples; earlier optional vivid examples are retained as retired history. The editorial language policy is now overridable. English guidance is retained; no new EN quality claim. Candidate release: local Gemma comparisons are separate evidence, not owner validation. The lock uses the engine revision pinned in CI.

## 0.3.0

Three original fictional before/after essay demonstrations and more concrete vivid
delivery guidance. RU essay examples use the explicit `examples.common` selector,
allowing child packs to supply a different example pool. No model quality claim.

## 0.2.0

Optional vivid delivery, platform-specific emoji guidance, profanity-free colloquial
language, and version placement for general-reader posts. Four original synthetic
examples. No personal or third-party corpus; no new LIVE quality claim.

## 0.1.0

Four soft defaults, four registers, RU/EN original positive examples and an optional
labelled negative example. Independent schema-compatible data pack; no personal sources.
