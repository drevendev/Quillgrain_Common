# Neutral editorial intent

Prefer a concrete explanation that earns the reader's attention. A paragraph may
add information, explain a mechanism, give an example, or make a useful transition.
Choose its length from that job. Let the topic supply emphasis; excitement is optional.

For notes, allow an unfinished thought. For essays, connect the detail to its implication.
For fiction, make room for perception, action and individual speech. For announcements,
explain what changed and retain the conditions under which it works.

These are designed defaults. The author may override a soft rule explicitly. Runtime
permissions, source boundaries and truth are enforced outside editorial preference.
